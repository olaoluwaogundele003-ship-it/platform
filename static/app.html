Schedules

Group cron jobs — what jobs do, but owned by the group. Runs in this same process.
tick 15s Daily 07:00 inspect · 12:00 match · 17:00 follow-up · weekly report. Pause, run-now, or add your own.
How it works

A schedule = when (once / interval / daily HH:MM / weekly / cron M H * * *) + what (inspect, match, follow_up, report, push_inbox, create_task, run_agent). Each firing writes validated results and triggers Flows via schedule.fired.

Flows

Redirects chain one step into the next — no human advancing the process.
Triggers: task.accepted · task.completed · offer.accepted · form.submitted · status.confirmed · schedule.fired · member.joined
